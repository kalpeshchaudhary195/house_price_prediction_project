Features & Model Architecture

Discover the 9 key features analyzed by our California Housing regression model to evaluate property values accurately.

💵

1. Median Income (MedInc)

Median household income in tens of thousands of USD. The single highest-correlated predictor with house prices in California census blocks.

Importance: Highest (★ ★ ★ ★ ★)
⏳

2. House Age (HouseAge)

Median age of structures in the block group in years. Influences depreciation, structural integrity, and architectural heritage.

Importance: Moderate (★ ★ ★)
🛋️

3. Average Rooms (AveRooms)

Average number of total rooms per household unit. Reflects living space, layout breadth, and overall floor area.

Importance: High (★ ★ ★ ★)
🛏️

4. Average Bedrooms (AveBedrms)

Average number of dedicated bedrooms per home. Evaluates family accommodation capacity vs. living space ratio.

Importance: Moderate (★ ★ ★)
👥

5. Block Population (Population)

Total resident count in the census tract block group. Helps detect high-density urban corridors vs quiet suburban zones.

Importance: Moderate (★ ★ ★)
👨‍👩‍👧

6. Average Occupancy (AveOccup)

Average household size. High density per unit typically correlates with rental properties or multi-family residences.

Importance: Moderate (★ ★ ★)
📍

7. Latitude Coordinate

North-South coordinate in California (32.0° to 43.0°). Distinguishes Southern California (LA, San Diego) from Northern California (Bay Area).

Importance: High (★ ★ ★ ★)
🌐

8. Longitude Coordinate

East-West coordinate in California (-125.0° to -114.0°). Captures coastal corridors versus interior desert/valley zones.

Importance: High (★ ★ ★ ★)
🌊

9. Ocean Proximity

Categorical proximity (NEAR BAY, NEAR OCEAN, 1H OCEAN, INLAND, ISLAND). Premium coastlines command substantial price surges.

Importance: Critical (★ ★ ★ ★ ★)
